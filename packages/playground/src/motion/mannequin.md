# mannequin.glb

A plain human mannequin without clothes or texture, rigged for MediaPipe with 60 bones (hips, spine, chest, upper chest,
neck, head, jaw and eyes; clavicles, upper arms and forearms with twist bones, hands and three-bone fingers; thighs, shins,
feet and toes). It was generated for this project and given to it for the motion bench, which poses and renders it to
test the tracker and solver against known bones.
