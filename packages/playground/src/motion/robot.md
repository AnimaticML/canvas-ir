# robot.glb

RobotExpressive, a rigged robot by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius), CC0 1.0. Converted to glTF, with three facial expression morph targets added and materials tidied, by Don McCurdy, as published in the three.js examples (`examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive`). The motion lab drives its skeleton from the solved body.
