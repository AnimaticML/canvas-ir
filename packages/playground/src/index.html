Selection

Click something on the board.

Drag moves an object, or one shape with Alt held; it snaps to other objects (see Snapping) unless Ctrl or Cmd is held. The knob, slider and button drag, click and take arrow keys themselves. Shift-click adds to the selection, and Shift-drag on empty space draws a lasso. Images hit-test, snap and wrap text by their opaque pixels; select the orange sticker and press Vectorize image to turn it into fills. Delete removes the selection. Scroll zooms; drag empty space to pan.

Snapping

Text laid out around what you drag is never a target, since it moves as you drag.

Rendering

Nothing drawn yet.

Only what changed is redrawn. While you drag, what lies below and above is cached as two layers. Add 10,000 shapes and zoom out to try it on a big board; small text turns into gray bars and specks are skipped when simplifying.
